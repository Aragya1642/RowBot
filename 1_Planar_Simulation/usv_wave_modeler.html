Model. Surge coordinate x, boat rides the surface (z = η, pitch = arctan ∂η/∂x). (m+mₐ)v̇ = F_thrust − ½C_d(v−u)|v−u| − m·g·sinθ·cosθ + F_wave. Waves use deep-water dispersion ω²=gk; irregular seas are a JONSWAP spectrum (γ=3.3). Gravity along the wave slope is the load your controller must beat — when a crest's slope demands more than ±Fₘₐₓ, no gain tuning saves you and the vessel stalls.
